# 08 · Working agreements (Antonio)

- **No patchwork.** When something must be redone, remove what was built for it and rebuild clean from source. No layering fixes.
- **Real props, real detail.** Furnish like Rockstar; personality over emptiness. Tables have legroom; sinks sit in counters; nothing floats.
- **Bathrooms and TVs fit the space.** Offices get separate men's and women's restrooms; penthouses get big screens where they make
  sense (living, media, bedrooms, master bath) and bathrooms that feel luxurious.
- **Seating must work** (sit-tested height, offset, scenario). **Doors are native** and lockable.
- **Every MLO ships its data file** (JSON + README): street door, seats, zones, interactions, TVs.
- **Room-by-room sign-off** with side-by-side screenshots on photo-matched builds.
- **Show, then report — in video.** The hand-off is a fly-through video of every changed room plus short action clips (swimming, doors, lifts), not screenshots. Screenshots are for my own checks. Review the frames yourself and say what is wrong before he has to.
- **Keep working**; don't stop to ask what's obviously next. Ask only when a choice is genuinely his.
- **Never wait for him on the rig**: kick or close his client and restart whenever needed, unless told otherwise.
- Safety: never print licence keys, txAdmin credentials or tokens; never push from test copies; never touch the live VPS.
